# Roof dial: first results (run 2026-08-20, corrected 2026-08-21)

Phases 1 and 2 plus a first cut of Phase 3, run the same day the scope landed. All queries
read-only on Metabase db 235; definitions per SCOPE.md; every grid cross-checks against the
probe numbers in `sql/probes_20260820.sql`. Windows: curve = bound NB dwellings created
May 1 to Aug 15 (3.5 months); harm = April+ binds, 0-90 day window, on the frozen 8/16
event snapshots.

**Revised 2026-08-21** after a senior data-science pressure test. Three headline numbers were
withdrawn and two arithmetic slips fixed; the corrections are listed at the end and the
affected numbers are marked *(withdrawn)* or *(corrected)* in place. **Treat this as an
exploratory read, not an approval artifact.** Nothing here identifies an optimal production
bar, and nothing here demonstrates that applying the exclusion prevents harm.

## The read

**80, 83 and 85 are candidate settings for a version-pinned shadow test on 101+ homes.
No one of them is defensible as a production bar yet, because there is no agreed value for
one caught home against one unwanted exclusion.** The priced candidates:

| candidate bar | capture (of 1,018 hand-applies) | 95% CI | running over per catch | cost of the step down to here | over-applies/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 85 | 50.7% (516) | 47.6-53.8% | 0.83 | 1.52 (from 90) | ~123 |
| **83 = the bottom-20% cut** | **54.7% (557)** | **51.6-57.7%** | **0.95** | **2.41 (from 85)** | **~151** |
| 80 | 60.7% (618); 58.0% at 0.87 on v1.2.0 only | 57.7-63.7% | 1.10 | 2.52 (from 83) | ~195 |

*Two per-month bases exist in this memo and they differ ~2x at bar 80: the column above
divides the 3.5-month bound-book counts (~195/mo), while the go-forward section's ~98/mo
projects the censored Aug 1-15 pool. Quote one, say which.*

- **There is no knee at 80-85** *(corrected)*. The earlier claim that "below 80 each extra
  catch costs 1.3 to 2 over-applies" confused the running average with the cost of the next
  step. On the marginal curve the step cost is already 2.41 at 83 and 2.52 at 80, and the
  only real change in slope in the candidate region is at 85 (1.52 -> 2.41); the curve
  steepens again below 75. See the marginal table in Phase 1.
- **The bottom-20% cut lands on bar 83**: the 80th-percentile score of the pooled 101+ bound
  book is exactly 83. That is a capacity heuristic on a pooled, bound-only sample, not an
  optimum, and it must be recomputed on v1.2.0-only eligible traffic before it is quoted -
  old versions score hot, so the 20% point may move.
- **Keep the dial to 101+.** Score separation is identical in 80-90 and 91-100 (hand median
  83-85 vs left-alone 44-49 everywhere), but hand-applies are 4-6x rarer below 101, so the
  same bar costs 2.5 to 9 over-applies per catch there (101+ is 83% of all 80+ hand-applies).
- **The curve is pooled across model versions, not stated against v1.2.0** *(corrected)*.
  Only the bar-80 row carries a v1.2.0 cut. At bar 80, pooled capture is 60.7% vs 58.0% on
  v1.2.0 while pooled over-per-catch is 1.10 vs 0.87 - so pooling inflates the apparent
  benefit and the apparent cost by different amounts. It is **not** uniformly a conservative
  floor, and 83 has never actually been priced on v1.2.0 alone.
- **The harm-prevention percentages are gone from this table** *(withdrawn)*. They were
  measured on a population with no age restriction and cannot be set beside 101+-only costs.
  See Phase 2.

Checks that held: adding April to the window moves capture by at most 3pp (the answer does
not depend on the window). Launch-state and non-launch curves match within ~5pp; the one
systematic gap (lower capture at 85-90 in launch states) is the automation already skimming
the highest scorers there. Quantiles reproduce the probe exactly (hand 71/85/94/97, left
alone 28/45/70/84). Wilson intervals on capture are tight (+/- ~3pp), so the separation is
not small-sample noise - but tight intervals do not repair the cohort problems below.

**Two structural limits on this curve, both open:**

1. **Bound-only, so it is a survivor sample.** `sql/01_bound_book.sql` filters to
   `quote_status = 'Issued'`, but the exclusion goes on *before* bind and can change whether
   a quote binds at all. This curve describes bound survivors under historical underwriting,
   not the traffic a wider rule would meet. Monthly over-apply volume is therefore likely
   understated. Fix: rebuild at score/decision time over all eligible quotes, keeping
   bind/non-bind as an outcome.
2. **"Hand-apply" vs "left alone" is a label of today's workflow, not ground truth.** The two
   groups differ in whether an underwriter looked at the roof at all. Capture measures how
   well the score imitates current behaviour, which is worth knowing and is not the same as
   accuracy.

Younger bands, for the record (same bar 80): 91-100 captures 57.0% at 4.23 over per catch;
80-90 captures 55.4% at 4.86. That is the case for keeping the dial at 101+.

## Phase 2: the harm overlay - WRONG POPULATION FOR THE 101+ RULE *(withdrawn as a headline)*

(April+ binds, 90-day window; 633 roof NOEs / 106 roof NOCs)

**Read this section only as a whole-book description.** `sql/02_postbind_roof_outcomes.sql`
has no dwelling-age filter, and `roof_dial/overlay.py` cannot segment by age, so the shares
below are computed over the entire snapshot book - which this same analysis found is 88%
driven by the under-91 clean lane. A rule restricted to 101+ homes cannot catch the events

| 60 | 434 | 68.6% | 77 | 72.6% |
| 70 | 349 | 55.1% | 65 | 61.3% |

| 90 | 143 | 22.6% | 22 | 20.8% |

- **The honest 101+ figure is much smaller: on already-reviewed 101+ binds only ~27% of
  post-bind leaks scored 80+**, against the 38.5% whole-book number. Use 27% until Phase 2
  is rebuilt on exactly-eligible 101+ policies.
- **What a bar-80 dial would still leave: 389 roof NOEs and 59 roof NOCs** of the April+
  book (61.5% and 55.7%). Post-bind review keeps most of its job at any bar above 70.
- NOC shares are thin: 44.3% at bar 80 has a 95% CI of 35.2-53.8%, and 28.3% at bar 85 runs
  20.6-37.5%. Do not read a difference between adjacent bars off this table.
- The shares are stable between the 90-day and 60-day windows (38.5% vs 38.8% at bar 80),
  so the ~30% runway censoring distorts counts more than the visible share - but "similar at
  60 and 90 days" is not a substitute for a mature cohort.
- Prevention concentrates in the unreviewed general book (U91_clean: 88% of NOE volume,
  39% catchable at 80). Small cells: cohort NOC rows are single digits outside U91.
- **Known defect in the extract:** the bind-time record is built from independent
  `max(score)`, `max(exclusion)`, `max(flag)`, `max(decision)` across a policy's
  New Business rows, with no issued-version selection. At ~1.16 dwellings per quote that can
  stitch together a home that never existed - the highest score from one dwelling with the
  coverage state of another. Direction of error unknown. Fix: pin the canonical as-of-bind
  version and carry the dwelling key.

## Phase 3: over-apply disposition - WITHDRAWN *(do not quote)*

| bar | over-applies | late catch | true disagreement | presumed false positive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 3,754 | 10.4% | 14.3% | 75.3% |
| 75 | 3,037 | 11.1% | 15.0% | 73.9% |
| 80 | 2,326 | 11.8% | 15.7% | 72.5% |
| 85 | 1,685 | 11.8% | 14.8% | 73.5% |

**The "72-75% are false positives" figure is withdrawn, and calling it a ceiling was too
generous.** The problem is not that the census is incomplete - it is that the negative label
is invalid. `sql/03_overapply_disposition.sql` draws harm only from the frozen three-slice
snapshot, but its `bound` denominator is every issued age-80+ policy from April onward, with
no snapshot-cohort membership and no 90-day maturity requirement. Every policy outside the
outcome census, and every policy still inside its runway, is therefore *structurally labelled
clean* and lands in the false-positive bucket. `roof_dial/overlay.py` has the same gap: no
outcome-observation eligibility state.

Also unresolved: `requires_uw_review` means "an alert required a look", not "an underwriter
reviewed this roof". Phase 3 reads it as real underwriter disagreement, which it cannot bear
without roof-specific review evidence.

Fix before this table is quoted: join every candidate to an explicit outcome-cohort manifest
and follow-up end date, and classify each as *harm observed*, *mature clean*,
*right-censored*, or *outside the outcome census*. Only mature, in-census policies may enter
a false-positive denominator.

## The NOC conversion question (added 8/20, corrected and reframed 8/21)

"Would those NOCs actually not have happened?" Two cuts on the April+ cohort book:

| bind score band | bound, no exclusion | roof NOCs (per 100) | bound WITH exclusion at bind | roof NOCs (per 100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 90+ | 1,167 | 21 (1.80) | 214 | 0 (0.00) |
| 83-89 | 785 | 16 (2.04) | 58 | 1 (1.72) |
| below 83 | 17,594 | 68 (0.39) | 230 | 0 |

**Counts** *(corrected)*: 38 of the 106 roof-cancelled homes scored 83+ (35.8%), not 39. Of
those, 37 were newly catchable (34.9%) - one already carried the exclusion and was cancelled
anyway. Same whole-book caveat as Phase 2: these cuts come from the age-unfiltered extract
with the independent-max() bind row, so a 101+-only rule reaches only the 101+ subset of the
38 - the reachable share is lower than 35.8%.

**This is association, not prevention.** The earlier wording ("would prevent", "most convert
to keep-with-exclusion", "the pilot confirms the conversion rate") is withdrawn. There is no
pilot. Three reasons to hold the claim:

1. **Selection.** The with-exclusion group was chosen by underwriters using imagery, severity
   and eligibility signals the score does not see, and it conditions on binding *after* the
   exclusion was applied.
2. **The comparison is not significant on its own terms.** Two-sided Fisher exact: 0/214 vs
   21/1,167 at 90+ gives p = 0.061; 1/58 vs 16/785 at 83-89 gives p = 1.00; pooled 83+
   (1/272 vs 37/1,952) gives p = 0.078. The 0/214 cell has a 95% upper bound of 1.8%, which
   is the same order as the 1.80 per 100 it is being compared against.
3. **Part of the effect is definitional, which is the sharper problem.** A roof NOC is a
   cancellation for roof condition and a roof NOE is the later addition of the roof
   exclusion. If the roof is already excluded at bind there is little left to cancel for and
   nothing left to endorse - so a drop in both is close to guaranteed by construction, and
   observing it tells us little about whether our actual exposure fell.

**The better pilot question, therefore, is not "does the exclusion stop roof
cancellations".** It nearly must. It is: *do the agent and the customer accept it* - bind
rate, abandonment, override requests, post-bind removals, complaints - and *is our exposure
reduced or only moved into the contract*, which needs the loss join.

What does stand without a causal claim: **today's setting provides effectively no
cancellation protection. The model said "exclude" on 0 of the 106 roof-cancelled homes,
including the 12 that scored 95+** (the decision's non-score inputs filtered every one out),
and only 1 of 106 carried the exclusion at bind. High-scoring roof cancellations sat almost
entirely among homes without the exclusion, in the territory a wider bar would cover.

## Texas and go-forward volumes

- **Texas cannot be the pilot read**: 52 bound 101+ dwellings in 3.5 months (6 hand-applies),
  and **the automation has never fired in Texas** (0 auto-applies on the 80+ book since the
  May 11 launch) - worth a config check with the model owner. Long-bake reads should use
  CA/NJ/PA, which dominate the launch-state 101+ book.
- **No absorption yet anywhere**: the other six launch states sit flat at 20-22 hand-applies
  per 100 bound 101+ homes May through July (August dips are right-censoring).
- **Go-forward pool (Aug 1-15 x2, nationwide ex CO/RI/WV, floor because of censoring):**
  ~208 hand-applies/mo on 80+ (~174 in 101+); today's automation replaces ~14/mo. At bar 80
  on 101+: ~114 catches/mo against ~98 over-applies/mo (July full-month ran ~1.5x these).
  These rates divide a two-week creation cohort by a supplied month count and select August
  on fast conversion; recompute by true issued date with a fixed maturation lag.
- **Never-referred compliance lane** *(corrected)*: at bar 80, ~35% of over-applies would
  carry an auto-exclusion on a quote no underwriter ever saw. On the proposed 101+ bar-80
  setting that is **~34 quotes/mo**, not the ~58 stated earlier - 58 was 35% of the wider
  80+ population and does not belong next to a 101+ recommendation. The 35% share itself
  was measured on the 80+ base; within 101+ (which the dwelling-age alert routes to review
  more often) the never-referred share is plausibly lower, so ~34 is a soft estimate. The share rises with a
  stricter bar (40% at 85). This is the filings/disclosure watch-item.

## What is still open

1. **A version-pinned, decision-time Phase 1 re-cut**: 101+, current deployable states,
   v1.2.0 only, built over all eligible quotes rather than bound survivors, with the
   bottom-20% point re-derived and marginal costs reported by band. Cluster at policy level.
   This is the one that decides whether 83 survives as the capacity point.
2. **Phase 2 rebuilt on exactly-eligible 101+ policies**, with a canonical as-of-bind version
   instead of the independent-`max()` stitch, and results reported at both policy and
   dwelling grain.
3. **The cohort rebuild plus an outcome-eligibility state** (harm observed / mature clean /
   right-censored / outside census), which is what makes any disposition table quotable.
4. **The exchange rate.** No bar can be chosen without a value for one caught home against
   one unwanted exclusion, plus its bind-loss, removal, complaint and compliance costs. The
   dwelling-alert framework already records this tolerance as unset; that is the decision ask,
   not approval of a number.
5. Validate `requires_uw_review` against actual roof review/action events before any
   "underwriter disagreed" reading.
6. Confirm the automation attribution is event-sourced rather than inferred from
   `flag='yes' AND decision='exclude'` plus coverage state.
7. The pushback re-pull (~Oct): post-bind removals of auto-applied exclusions are still n=1.
8. The Curry photo session on 10-20 quotes just above the candidate bar.
9. Implementation translation with the model owner: the live decision uses inputs beyond the
   score, so any bar must be mapped into the shipped rule (and the Texas zero-fire checked).
10. Commit the exact executable SQL and dated aggregate extracts for every table above -
    several currently live only in the agents' runs, so the memo is not independently
    reproducible from this folder.
