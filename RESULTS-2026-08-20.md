# Roof dial: first results (run 2026-08-20)

Phases 1 and 2 plus a first cut of Phase 3, run the same day the scope landed. All queries
read-only on Metabase db 235; definitions per SCOPE.md; every grid cross-checks against the
probe numbers in `sql/probes_20260820.sql`. Windows: curve = bound NB dwellings created
May 1 to Aug 15 (3.5 months); harm = April+ binds, 0-90 day window, on the frozen 8/16
event snapshots.

## The read

**The defensible band is a score bar of 80 to 85, applied to 101+ homes only, stated
against model v1.2.0.** Underwriting picks the point; the priced candidates:

| candidate bar | capture (of 1,018 hand-applies) | over per catch | over-applies/mo | post-bind roof NOEs visible at bind | roof NOCs visible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 85 | 50.7% (516) | 0.83 | ~123 | 29.1% | 28.3% |
| **83 = Curry's bottom-20%** | **54.7% (557)** | **0.95** | **~151** | between the 80 and 85 rows | between |
| 80 | 60.7% (618); 58.0% at 0.87 on v1.2.0 only | 1.10 | ~195 | 38.5% | 44.3% |

- The knee is at 80-85: below 80 each extra catch costs 1.3 to 2 over-applies; above 85
  capture falls fast for little over-apply relief.
- **Curry's bottom-20% heuristic independently lands on bar 83** (the 80th-percentile score
  of the pooled 101+ bound book is exactly 83): 54.7% capture at 0.95 over per catch.
- **Keep the dial to 101+.** Score separation is identical in 80-90 and 91-100 (hand median
  83-85 vs left-alone 44-49 everywhere), but hand-applies are 4-6x rarer below 101, so the
  same bar costs 2.5 to 9 over-applies per catch there (101+ is 83% of all 80+ hand-applies).
- **Define the bar against v1.2.0.** Old versions (v1.1.x) score the book 5-10 points hot:
  at bar 80, over-apply share is 22.2% on v1.1.x vs 12.0% on v1.2.0. Pooled numbers here are
  a conservative floor for go-forward behavior.

## Phase 2: the harm overlay (April+ binds, 90-day window; 633 roof NOEs / 106 roof NOCs)

| bar | roof NOEs visible at bind | share of 633 | roof NOCs visible | share of 106 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 434 | 68.6% | 77 | 72.6% |
| 70 | 349 | 55.1% | 65 | 61.3% |
| 75 | 300 | 47.4% | 55 | 51.9% |
| 80 | 244 | 38.5% | 47 | 44.3% |
| 85 | 184 | 29.1% | 30 | 28.3% |
| 90 | 143 | 22.6% | 22 | 20.8% |

- **What a bar-80 dial would still miss: 389 roof NOEs and 59 roof NOCs** of the April+
  book (61.5% and 55.7%). Post-bind review keeps most of its job at any bar above 70.
- The shares are stable between the 90-day and 60-day windows (38.5% vs 38.8% at bar 80),
  so the ~30% runway censoring distorts counts, not the preventable share. NOCs land late
  (only 57 of 106 within 60 days).
- Prevention concentrates in the unreviewed general book (U91_clean: 88% of NOE volume,
  39% catchable at 80). On already-reviewed 101+ binds only ~27% of post-bind leaks scored
  80+; the reviewed lane's leftover harm is mostly invisible to any bar. Small cells:
  cohort NOC rows are single digits outside U91.

## Phase 3 first cut: over-apply disposition (snapshot cohorts, April+ binds; NOT a census of 80+)

| bar | over-applies | late catch | true disagreement | presumed false positive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70 | 3,754 | 10.4% | 14.3% | 75.3% |
| 75 | 3,037 | 11.1% | 15.0% | 73.9% |
| 80 | 2,326 | 11.8% | 15.7% | 72.5% |
| 85 | 1,685 | 11.8% | 14.8% | 73.5% |

- At bar 80, roughly 1 in 4 over-applies is vindicated (harm arrived within 90 days anyway)
  or was a real underwriter judgment call; ~72% are presumed false positives. That share is
  a **ceiling**: ~30% of these binds lack full runway, so late catches will grow as spring
  binds mature.
- The mix barely moves with the bar (72-75% across 70-85): raising the bar buys fewer
  over-applies, not a cleaner mix.
- **Population caveat, state it everywhere:** this table runs on the three snapshot cohort
  slices (dominated by the under-91 clean lane), not a census of bound 80+ homes. Re-run
  after the cohort rebuild before quoting as the Phase 3 answer.

## Texas and go-forward volumes

- **Texas cannot be the pilot read**: 52 bound 101+ dwellings in 3.5 months (6 hand-applies),
  and **the automation has never fired in Texas** (0 auto-applies on the 80+ book since the
  May 11 launch) — worth a config check with the model owner. Long-bake reads should use
  CA/NJ/PA, which dominate the launch-state 101+ book.
- **No absorption yet anywhere**: the other six launch states sit flat at 20-22 hand-applies
  per 100 bound 101+ homes May through July (August dips are right-censoring).
- **Go-forward pool (Aug 1-15 x2, nationwide ex CO/RI/WV, floor because of censoring):**
  ~208 hand-applies/mo on 80+ (~174 in 101+); today's automation replaces ~14/mo. At bar 80
  on 101+: ~114 catches/mo against ~98 over-applies/mo (July full-month ran ~1.5x these).
- **Never-referred compliance lane**: at bar 80, ~35% of over-applies (~58 quotes/mo at
  go-forward volume) would carry an auto-exclusion on a quote no underwriter ever saw; the
  share rises with a stricter bar (40% at 85). This is the filings/disclosure watch-item.
