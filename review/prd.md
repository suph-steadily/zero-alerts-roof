### Routing sensitivity from live quote traffic

`routing_compliance.sql` ran September 23. Among 10,157 unique August 2026 NB quotes with 11,600 valid 101+ homes (creation-year age, v1.2.0 current rows, CO/RI/WV excluded; America/Chicago dates), retaining current inferred auto coverage or adding score >=83 identifies 2,495 quotes/2,701 homes. Only six such quotes/homes lack both the current UW gate and any recorded referral note. Under full pass-through the 2,495/2,701 is an upper bound before other alerts, eligible forms and attestation responses. Of that selected traffic, 267 quotes/283 homes were observed bound before September 23. These are current-row routing proxies with uneven bind maturity, not incremental forecasts or event-sourced machine actions. At bars 80 and 85 the quote upper bounds are 2,902 and 2,222 on the same August denominator.

The query uses Chicago dates consistently. An initial mixed-timezone run produced a spurious September bucket: the session interpreted midnight literals in Chicago while the unconverted column grouped on its own timezone. That run is superseded. Original-source replications elsewhere retain their original predicates and flag the boundary limitation.
