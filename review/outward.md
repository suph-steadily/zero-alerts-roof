## State imagery requirements

The brief's shared January 1, 2027 date is not supported for all three states. These sources govern particular adverse actions and products; they do not by themselves establish eligibility for this landlord quote workflow.

* Kentucky Bulletin 2026-01 is dated March 11, 2026. It states the Department's current, nonbinding position on cancellations, nonrenewals and claim denials. It distinguishes satellite from aerial imagery and describes clarity, a written condition summary and a capture date within the preceding 12 months for aerial evidence. It does not announce a January 2027 start. [Kentucky bulletin, pp. 1-2](https://insurance.ky.gov/ppc/Documents/Bulletin%202026-01.pdf).
* Georgia HB 1344 Part VII addresses admitted owner-occupied homeowners policies, including imagery-based cancellation/nonrenewal, dated imagery, appeal and cure procedures. Section 16-1 sets January 1, 2027 as the effective date. That scope does not automatically cover this landlord-book proposal. [Enacted Georgia law, Part VII and section 16-1](https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2026-signed-legislation/hb-1344/download).
* Colorado's B-5.57 is identified in an April 7, 2026 notice, but the official PDF was blocked to this environment. Its exact effective date and product/action scope remain CANNOT VERIFY here. Do not repeat January 2027 as established. [Official bulletin link](https://doi.colorado.gov/sites/doi/files/documents/New%20Bulletin%205.57%20Aerial%20Imagery.pdf), [notice identifying the bulletin](https://aaisviews.aaisonline.com/compliance-alerts/colorado-issues-bulletin-regarding-the-use-of-aerial-imagery).

Map actual state, admitted status, product, action and image date before treating any of these as a launch rule. Image capture dates and an appeal path are measurement/design dependencies even when the cited statute does not apply to a particular form.
