# Bind-rate impact of the roof exclusion on 101+ homes

*Run 2026-08-25 by a three-analyst workflow, each adversarially verified by an independent recomputation agent. Verdict: **CONFIRMED** - every load-bearing number reproduced from independently written SQL. Metabase db 235, read-only.*

**Headline.** Applying the roof exclusion costs surprisingly little bind on 101+ homes: quotes an underwriter approved WITH the exclusion bind at 41.8% vs 48.0% for approved quotes without it, and on equally-bad roofs (score 90+) the gap shrinks to about 1.5 points (44.9% vs 46.4%, statistically indistinguishable) - what actually decides whether a 101+ quote binds is the referral gate itself (never-referred 101+ quotes bind ~1% with or without the exclusion).

## Key numbers

- **Bind rate, approved referred 101+ quotes WITH hand exclusion**: 41.8% (Wilson 95% CI 40.0-43.7) *(base: 1,102 bound / 2,635 approved referred quotes carrying a hand-applied exclusion; created Apr 1-Aug 11 2026)*
- **Bind rate, approved referred 101+ quotes with NO exclusion**: 48.0% (47.0-49.0) *(base: 4,479 / 9,333 approved referred no-exclusion quotes, same window)*
- **Same comparison on equally-bad roofs (score 90+)**: 44.9% with exclusion vs 46.4% without (CIs overlap) *(base: 482/1,074 vs 337/726 approved referred score-90+ quotes)*
- **Never-referred 101+ bind rate (the gate that actually decides)**: ~1% regardless of exclusion *(base: no-RSE 301/30,111 = 1.0%; auto-RSE 3/367 = 0.8%)*
- **Referred-only bind: hand-RSE vs no-RSE**: 35.1% vs 33.9% (indistinguishable) *(base: 1,113/3,170 vs 4,537/13,391 referred quotes)*
- **Auto-excluded score-90+ quotes get referred MORE, not less**: 37.8% vs 20.8% referral rate *(base: 199/527 auto vs 1,280/6,141 no-RSE score-90+ quotes)*
- **End-to-end bind on score-90+ quotes**: auto-excluded 9.7% vs untouched 5.9% *(base: 51/527 vs 364/6,141, full universe incl. never-referred)*
- **Reconciliation vs RESULTS-2026-08-20 memo**: 61 auto / 1,033 hand / 4,476 none (memo: 59/1,018/4,404; +1.4%, live-table late converters) *(base: bound 101+ dwellings created May 1-Aug 15 2026)*

## What is the bind-rate impact of the roof exclusion on 101+ homes?

**Setup, in plain terms.** We took every new-business quote with at least one 101+ year old dwelling, created April 1 through August 11, 2026 (the cutoff gives every quote at least 14 days to convert; most binds happen within 1-2 days). That is **47,435 unique quotes** (an earlier draft said 47,578; that double-counted 142 quotes with two statuses), of which 6,097 bound. Unlike the earlier roof-dial work, this universe includes the quotes that never bound - which is the whole point: the exclusion goes on before bind, so you can only see its bind effect if the non-binders are in the denominator. "Bind rate" = share of quotes reaching Issued. A quote counts as carrying the exclusion if any of its 101+ dwellings does (about 1.16 dwellings per quote; we aggregated to quote level because binding is a quote-level event). "Referred" = the agent submitted the quote to underwriting (real referral submission events, not the "an alert wanted a look" flag).

### 1. The raw three-way split (and why it misleads on its own)

| lane | quotes | bound | bind rate | Wilson 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) hand-applied exclusion (underwriter) | 3,348 | 1,200 | **35.8%** | 34.2-37.5% |
| (b) auto-applied exclusion (automation) | 585 | 59 | **10.1%** | 7.9-12.8% |
| (c) no exclusion | 43,502 | 4,838 | **11.1%** | 10.8-11.4% |

Read literally this says "hand-excluded quotes bind 3x MORE than untouched quotes," which is obviously selection, not cause. The decoder ring is the referral gate:

### 2. The referral gate dominates everything

101+ homes carry the dwelling-age review requirement, so **a 101+ quote essentially cannot bind without a referral**. Never-referred 101+ quotes bind at **1.0%** (301/30,111) with no exclusion and **0.8%** (3/367) with the auto-exclusion - identical. So:

- The hand lane binds high because hand-applies only happen inside referral review, i.e. on quotes whose agent already pushed them forward (94.7% of hand-RSE quotes were referred).
- The auto lane binds low mostly because **63% of auto-excluded quotes were never referred** (367/585) - and never-referred means ~0% bind for everyone on 101+, exclusion or not. The automation fires at quote creation on lots of casual drafts that were never going anywhere.
- The no-exclusion lane binds low because 69% of it was never referred.

**Within referred quotes only** (the first fair-ish comparison):

| lane (referred 101+ only) | quotes | bound | bind rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hand exclusion | 3,170 | 1,113 | 35.1% | 33.5-36.8% |
| auto exclusion | 218 | 56 | 25.7% | 20.3-31.9% |
| no exclusion | 13,391 | 4,537 | 33.9% | 33.1-34.7% |

Hand-excluded referred quotes bind the same as referred quotes with no exclusion (35.1% vs 33.9%). Auto-excluded referred quotes bind ~8 points lower - but those are, by construction, the worst-scoring roofs, which brings us to the decision cut.

### 3. The cleanest cut: what happens after the underwriter says yes

Nearly every referred quote got a decision (only 5 of 16,779 have none). Rejected quotes bind 0% in every lane (mechanical), and "general response" quotes bind 2-3% in every lane. So the live question is: **when underwriting approves, does the exclusion on the quote stop the agent/landlord from finishing?**

| approved referred 101+ quotes | quotes | bound | bind rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| approved WITH hand exclusion | 2,635 | 1,102 | **41.8%** | 40.0-43.7% |
| approved WITH auto exclusion | 127 | 56 | **44.1%** | 35.8-52.8% |
| approved, no exclusion | 9,333 | 4,479 | **48.0%** | 47.0-49.0% |

Raw answer: approved-with-exclusion quotes bind **6.2 points lower** (41.8 vs 48.0, non-overlapping CIs) - about **13% fewer binds** among approved quotes.

But the excluded quotes also have much worse roofs, and worse roofs bind less even without an exclusion. Holding roof quality roughly constant (roof score 90+, the band where the automation lives):

| approved referred 101+ quotes, score 90+ | quotes | bound | bind rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| approved WITH hand exclusion | 1,074 | 482 | 44.9% | 41.9-47.9% |
| approved WITH auto exclusion | 117 | 48 | 41.0% | 32.5-50.1% |
| approved, no exclusion | 726 | 337 | 46.4% | 42.8-50.1% |

On equally-bad roofs the gap collapses to **~1.5 points for hand-applies (44.9 vs 46.4) and ~5 points for auto (41.0 vs 46.4), and every interval overlaps**. So most of the raw 6.2-point gap is "these are worse houses," not "the exclusion scared the deal off."

**The honest bracket for the exclusion's own bind cost on an approved 101+ quote: somewhere between ~0 and ~6 points, best point estimate ~1.5-5 points, not statistically distinguishable from zero at current volumes.**

### 4. The auto lane specifically (Suph's rollout question)

- **No pre-referral scare-off.** Among score-90+ quotes, auto-excluded ones get referred nearly twice as often as untouched ones (37.8% vs 20.8%). If anything, the exclusion appearing on the quote prompts the agent to engage (possibly to ask for its removal).
- **End-to-end, score-matched, the auto-excluded quotes bind MORE**: 9.7% (51/527) vs 5.9% (364/6,141) for untouched 90+ quotes. The automation is not visibly suppressing bind anywhere we can measure.
- After approval, auto-excluded quotes bind 44.1% - right in line with everything else.

### 5. What the non-binds look like

There is no explicit "declined" or "expired" status in this table (statuses are Draft / Quoted / Issued / Invalidated), so abandonment vs decline can only be approximated:
- Non-binding quotes overwhelmingly sit in **Draft** (agent walked away or never finished): e.g. 29,686 of the 29,810 never-referred no-exclusion non-binds.
- **Rejected by underwriting** (from the decision events): 2,085 no-exclusion + 84 hand + 41 auto referred quotes, 0% of which bound.
- **Invalidated** (discarded) and **Quoted** (locked) are tiny everywhere (~1-2%).

### 6. Which comparison is closest to causal - and why none fully is

The closest is **approved referred quotes at score 90+, hand-exclusion vs no-exclusion (44.9% vs 46.4%)**: both groups passed the same referral gate, got the same decision, and sit in the same roof-quality band. It is still not causal because (i) inside the 90+ band, underwriters chose which roofs to exclude using imagery the score does not capture, so the excluded roofs are likely still worse; (ii) approval "with exclusion" and approval "clean" may come with different premiums and conditions; (iii) an agent who hated the exclusion may have abandoned before the decision, which this cut cannot see (though the referral-rate evidence in section 4 points the other way). A true read needs the shadow test / pilot the roof-dial memo already calls for.

### 7. Reconciliation and oddities

- **Reconciles with the 8/20 memo:** bound 101+ dwellings May 1 - Aug 15 = 61 auto / 1,033 hand / 4,476 left alone vs the memo's 59 / 1,018 / 4,404 (+1.4%; the table is live and late converters bound after 8/20). 
- **One unexplained pocket:** 178 quotes carry a hand-type exclusion but have no referral submission at all, and bind at 48.9%. We checked: none of their policies has any referred sibling quote, and none has multiple quotes, so it is not requote inheritance within a policy. Most likely underwriter/CX touches that reach the quote outside the referral-note lane, or requotes of previously-referred quotes that land on a new policy id. 5% of the hand lane; does not change any conclusion.

### Bottom line for Suph

The fear "if we auto-apply the exclusion, agents will stop binding old homes" does not show up in the data. On 101+ homes the referral gate, and then the underwriting decision, decide bind; the exclusion itself moves the post-approval close rate by at most a few points and possibly nothing. The measured acceptance costs of widening the auto-exclusion look small; the compliance lane (auto-exclusions on never-seen quotes) and post-bind pushback remain the open cost items, not bind rate.

## Caveats

- Nothing here is causal. The closest-to-causal cut (approved referred score-90+ quotes, exclusion vs not: 44.9% vs 46.4%) still cannot rule out that underwriters excluded the worse roofs WITHIN the 90+ band, that approval terms/premiums differed, or that some agents abandoned before a decision. The shadow test / pilot the roof-dial memo calls for is still the real answer.
- Exclusion state is read off the quote's current (latest) row, not a decision-time snapshot. On a bound quote that is the issued state; on an abandoned draft it is the last state before abandonment. We cannot order 'exclusion applied, then agent walked' vs 'agent walked, then nothing' on drafts. Mitigation: agents cannot set this coverage, so a non-auto exclusion on a draft implies an underwriter touched it, which almost always means a referral was in flight.
- Lane definitions: auto = exclusion selected AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion='yes' AND roof_condition_score_decision='exclude' on the same row (no cross-column stitching); hand = exclusion selected without that signature. Attribution is inferred, not event-sourced (same open item 6 as the RESULTS memo). A hand-removal of an auto-apply would be misfiled, believed rare.
- Quote-grain aggregation: a quote qualifies if ANY dwelling is 101+; the lane is the max across its 101+ dwelling rows; bind is quote-level (Issued). Dwelling-only 101+ filtering means an exclusion sitting on a younger sibling dwelling of a mixed quote is not counted.
- Maturation: quotes created through Aug 11 get >=14 days to convert (median time-to-bind is ~1-2 days). A few very slow converters may still flip Draft->Issued, which would nudge all bind rates up slightly and roughly equally.
- Referral = agent submission events (uw_note_submitted_and_review_requested, category underwriting_review) from the eventstore, deduped by quote. 178 hand-exclusion quotes (5% of the hand lane, binding 48.9%) have no such event and no referred sibling quote on their policy; likely UW/CX touches outside the referral-note lane or requotes landing on a new policy id. Unexplained but too small to change conclusions.
- The window (Apr 1 - Aug 11) mixes flag-on and flag-off states for the auto lane (waves Apr 21/Apr 30/May 11, nationwide ~Jul 30, never fired in TX, CO/RI/WV off). The no-exclusion 90+ comparison group therefore contains flag-off-state bad roofs the automation never saw; state-pinned re-cuts were not run.
- Score bands pool model versions (v1.1.x scores 5-10 points hot vs v1.2.0), same limitation the memo flags for the aperture curve.
- Non-bind breakdown is approximate: quote_status has no decline/expiry value (Draft/Quoted/Invalidated only); UW rejection comes from decision events and overlaps the status buckets.
- 'Approved' = latest decision event is approval; a quote approved after earlier general responses counts as approved. Conditionally-approved appeared as zero rows in this window's mapping (the LIKE '%approved' pattern would also absorb it; the separate cond_approved label matched none).

## SQL

```sql
-- All queries: Metabase db 235 (ClickHouse), read-only. Universe = NB quotes with >=1 dwelling aged 101+
-- (age = 2026 - pol_prop_year_built, year_built > 1700), created 2026-04-01 .. 2026-08-11 (14-day maturation).

-- ============================================================
-- Q0: grain + status sanity (rows = quote x dwelling exactly; RSE exists on non-bound quotes)
-- Result: Draft 41,072 q / Invalidated 203 / Issued 6,097 / Quoted 206; Draft rows carry 2,918 RSE (550 auto)
-- ============================================================
SELECT
    quote_status,
    count()                                        AS rows_,
    uniqExact(quote_id)                            AS quotes,
    uniqExact((quote_id, dwelling_id))             AS quote_dwellings,
    countIf(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected') AS rse_rows,
    countIf(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
            AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
            AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude') AS auto_rse_rows
FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
  AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-04-01 00:00:00')
  AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-12 00:00:00')
  AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
  AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
GROUP BY quote_status
ORDER BY quote_status;

-- ============================================================
-- Q1: main table - bind rate by RSE lane x referral, quote grain
-- (note: 'action' is inside the JSON payload, not a column - a first attempt with a bare
--  `action = ...` predicate failed with UNKNOWN_IDENTIFIER and was corrected as below)
-- Result rows (lane, referred, quotes, bound, nb_draft, nb_quoted, nb_invalidated):
--   hand,0: 178, 87, 52, 0, 39   | hand,1: 3170, 1113, 2003, 45, 9
--   auto,0: 367, 3, 364, 0, 0    | auto,1: 218, 56, 160, 2, 0
--   none,0: 30111, 301, 29686, 0, 124 | none,1: 13391, 4537, 8667, 156, 31
-- ============================================================
WITH refs AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT JSONExtractString(data, 'quote_id') AS quote_id
    FROM raw_pg_eventstore.eventstore_good_events
    WHERE name = 'underwriting_review_note'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'action') = 'uw_note_submitted_and_review_requested'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'category') = 'underwriting_review'
      AND created_at >= toDateTime('2026-03-01 00:00:00')
      AND created_at <  toDateTime('2026-08-26 00:00:00')
),
q AS (
    SELECT quote_id,
           max(quote_status = 'Issued')                                   AS issued,
           any(quote_status)                                              AS status,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected', 1, 0))  AS rse_any,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
                  AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
                  AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude', 1, 0)) AS auto_any
    FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
    WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
      AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-04-01 00:00:00')
      AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-12 00:00:00')
      AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
      AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
    GROUP BY quote_id
)
SELECT
    multiIf(q.auto_any = 1, 'b_auto_rse',
            q.rse_any = 1,  'a_hand_rse',
            'c_no_rse')                                    AS lane,
    if(q.quote_id IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs), 1, 0)    AS referred,
    count()                                                AS quotes,
    sum(issued)                                            AS bound,
    countIf(status = 'Draft'       AND issued = 0)         AS nb_draft,
    countIf(status = 'Quoted'      AND issued = 0)         AS nb_quoted,
    countIf(status = 'Invalidated' AND issued = 0)         AS nb_invalidated
FROM q
GROUP BY lane, referred
ORDER BY lane, referred;

-- ============================================================
-- Q2: referred quotes only - lane x latest UW decision x bind (+ score-90+ sub-cells)
-- Result (lane, outcome, quotes, bound, score90plus, bound_score90plus):
--   hand approved 2635,1102,1074,482 | hand GR 451,11,193,5 | hand rejected 84,0,36,0
--   auto approved 127,56,117,48      | auto GR 50,0,49,0    | auto rejected 41,0,33,0
--   none approved 9333,4479,726,337  | none GR 1968,58,258,6| none rejected 2085,0,294,0 | none no_decision 5,0,2,0
-- ============================================================
WITH refs AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT JSONExtractString(data, 'quote_id') AS quote_id
    FROM raw_pg_eventstore.eventstore_good_events
    WHERE name = 'underwriting_review_note'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'action') = 'uw_note_submitted_and_review_requested'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'category') = 'underwriting_review'
      AND created_at >= toDateTime('2026-03-01 00:00:00')
      AND created_at <  toDateTime('2026-08-26 00:00:00')
),
dec AS (
    SELECT JSONExtractString(data, 'quote_id') AS quote_id,
           argMax(JSONExtractString(data, 'action'), created_at) AS last_action
    FROM raw_pg_eventstore.eventstore_good_events
    WHERE name = 'underwriting_review_note'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'category') = 'underwriting_review'
      AND (JSONExtractString(data, 'action') LIKE 'uw_review_decision_%'
           OR JSONExtractString(data, 'action') LIKE 'uw_immediate_decision_%')
      AND created_at >= toDateTime('2026-03-01 00:00:00')
      AND created_at <  toDateTime('2026-08-26 00:00:00')
    GROUP BY quote_id
),
q AS (
    SELECT quote_id,
           max(quote_status = 'Issued')                                   AS issued,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected', 1, 0))  AS rse_any,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
                  AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
                  AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude', 1, 0)) AS auto_any,
           max(pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_condition_score)    AS max_score
    FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
    WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
      AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-04-01 00:00:00')
      AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-12 00:00:00')
      AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
      AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
    GROUP BY quote_id
)
SELECT
    multiIf(q.auto_any = 1, 'b_auto_rse',
            q.rse_any = 1,  'a_hand_rse',
            'c_no_rse')                                       AS lane,
    multiIf(d.last_action = '',                                'no_decision',
            d.last_action LIKE '%conditionally_approved',      'cond_approved',
            d.last_action LIKE '%approved',                    'approved',
            d.last_action LIKE '%general_response',            'general_response',
            d.last_action LIKE '%rejected',                    'rejected',
            'other')                                          AS uw_outcome,
    count()                                                   AS quotes,
    sum(q.issued)                                             AS bound,
    countIf(q.max_score >= 90)                                AS score90plus,
    sumIf(q.issued, q.max_score >= 90)                        AS bound_score90plus
FROM q
LEFT JOIN dec AS d ON d.quote_id = q.quote_id
WHERE q.quote_id IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs)
GROUP BY lane, uw_outcome
ORDER BY lane, uw_outcome;

-- ============================================================
-- Q3: full universe - lane x score band x referred (pre-referral deterrence check)
-- Result (lane, band, quotes, referred, bound, bound_referred):
--   hand 90+: 1394,1303,534,487 | hand <90: 1924,1858,656,621 | hand unscored: 30,9,10,5
--   auto 90+: 527,199,51,48     | auto <90: 58,19,8,8
--   none 90+: 6141,1280,364,343 | none <90: 35100,12084,4418,4178 | none unscored: 2261,27,56,16
-- ============================================================
WITH refs AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT JSONExtractString(data, 'quote_id') AS quote_id
    FROM raw_pg_eventstore.eventstore_good_events
    WHERE name = 'underwriting_review_note'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'action') = 'uw_note_submitted_and_review_requested'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'category') = 'underwriting_review'
      AND created_at >= toDateTime('2026-03-01 00:00:00')
      AND created_at <  toDateTime('2026-08-26 00:00:00')
),
q AS (
    SELECT quote_id,
           max(quote_status = 'Issued')                                   AS issued,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected', 1, 0))  AS rse_any,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
                  AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
                  AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude', 1, 0)) AS auto_any,
           max(pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_condition_score)    AS max_score
    FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
    WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
      AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-04-01 00:00:00')
      AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-12 00:00:00')
      AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
      AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
    GROUP BY quote_id
)
SELECT
    multiIf(q.auto_any = 1, 'b_auto_rse',
            q.rse_any = 1,  'a_hand_rse',
            'c_no_rse')                                    AS lane,
    multiIf(q.max_score IS NULL, 'unscored',
            q.max_score >= 90,   '90+',
            'below90')                                     AS score_band,
    count()                                                AS quotes,
    countIf(q.quote_id IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs))     AS referred,
    sum(q.issued)                                          AS bound,
    sumIf(q.issued, q.quote_id IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs)) AS bound_referred
FROM q
GROUP BY lane, score_band
ORDER BY lane, score_band;

-- ============================================================
-- Q4: reconciliation vs RESULTS-2026-08-20.md (bound 101+ DWELLINGS, May 1 - Aug 15)
-- Result: auto 61 / hand 1,033 / none 4,476 (memo: 59 / 1,018 / 4,404; +1.4%, live-table late converters)
-- ============================================================
SELECT
    multiIf(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
            AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
            AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude', 'auto',
            prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected', 'hand',
            'none') AS grp,
    count() AS dwellings
FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness' AND quote_status = 'Issued'
  AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-05-01 00:00:00')
  AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-16 00:00:00')
  AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
  AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
GROUP BY grp
ORDER BY grp;

-- ============================================================
-- Q5: the 178 hand-RSE never-referred quotes - requote-inheritance check
-- (a correlated-subquery version failed with UNKNOWN_IDENTIFIER; rewritten as a join)
-- Result: 178 quotes / 87 bound / 0 policies ever referred on any sibling quote / 0 policies with >1 NB quote
-- ============================================================
WITH refs AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT JSONExtractString(data, 'quote_id') AS quote_id
    FROM raw_pg_eventstore.eventstore_good_events
    WHERE name = 'underwriting_review_note'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'action') = 'uw_note_submitted_and_review_requested'
      AND JSONExtractString(data, 'category') = 'underwriting_review'
      AND created_at >= toDateTime('2025-06-01 00:00:00')
      AND created_at <  toDateTime('2026-08-26 00:00:00')
),
q AS (
    SELECT quote_id,
           any(policy_id)                                                 AS policy_id,
           max(quote_status = 'Issued')                                   AS issued,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected', 1, 0))  AS rse_any,
           max(if(prop_cov_roof_surfacing_exclusion = 'selected'
                  AND pol_ff_automated_roof_exclusion = 'yes'
                  AND pol_prop_steadily_roof_condition_score_decision = 'exclude', 1, 0)) AS auto_any
    FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
    WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
      AND pol_created_timestamp >= toDateTime('2026-04-01 00:00:00')
      AND pol_created_timestamp <  toDateTime('2026-08-12 00:00:00')
      AND pol_prop_year_built > 1700
      AND 2026 - pol_prop_year_built >= 101
    GROUP BY quote_id
),
target AS (
    SELECT quote_id, policy_id, issued
    FROM q
    WHERE rse_any = 1 AND auto_any = 0
      AND quote_id NOT IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs)
),
pol_flags AS (
    SELECT policy_id,
           max(if(quote_id IN (SELECT quote_id FROM refs), 1, 0)) AS pol_ever_referred,
           uniqExact(quote_id) AS nb_quotes_on_policy
    FROM dbt.ipod_standard_mga_raw_policy_info
    WHERE quote_type = 'NewBusiness'
      AND policy_id IN (SELECT policy_id FROM target)
    GROUP BY policy_id
)
SELECT
    count()                                   AS target_quotes,
    sum(t.issued)                             AS target_bound,
    sum(pf.pol_ever_referred)                 AS policy_ever_referred,
    sumIf(t.issued, pf.pol_ever_referred = 1) AS bound_and_policy_referred,
    countIf(pf.nb_quotes_on_policy > 1)       AS policy_has_multiple_nb_quotes
FROM target t
LEFT JOIN pol_flags pf ON pf.policy_id = t.policy_id;
```
